Connection and signal-level guidance
For XY, connect left to X and right to Y. For a single-input Y-T scope, select X only and connect the mono output to its input. Turn the volume up full — the level is the picture size, and anything the operating system does to the signal (EQ, "enhancements", spatial audio) will distort it.

Audio goes to your system's default output unless you pick another. Where supported, the device picker requests output access only. Otherwise, select the output in your system sound settings before starting. This page never requests microphone access.
